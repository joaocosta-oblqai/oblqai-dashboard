# OBLQAI Bot Pricing — Canon

**Status:** Locked · 2026-09-24 · Market: Portugal (EUR, ex-VAT)

Pricing for Grok-style AI bots we set up and run for business clients. Modular: clients pay per bot and per connector, plus one fixed platform fee.

## Price list

| Item | One-off setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Platform (one per client) | — | **€190** |
| Per bot | **€1,000** | **€100** |
| Per connector (standard or custom, flat) | **€300** | — |

- Connectors are priced flat at €300 setup whether we use a ready-made one or build a custom one. No monthly charge per connector.
- The platform fee covers hosting, monitoring, security / GDPR, support and updates.

## Formula

```
Setup   = €1,000 × bots + €300 × connectors
Monthly = €190 + €100 × bots
```

## Worked examples

| Client | Setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| 1 bot + 2 connectors | €1,600 | €290 |
| 3 bots + 5 connectors | €4,500 | €490 |
| 5 bots + 7 connectors | €7,100 | €690 |
